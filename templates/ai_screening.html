The preset automation that clears a watchlist record without an analyst, and what it did on this tenant's cases. A deterministic rule book, not a model: every rule is a check a compliance team can read and audit. A case where the rules clear every alert is auto-verified once it closes, with nothing more asked of the customer; until a customer's reply comes back it is waiting on customer. A case with anything left over is human-in-the-loop, and the analyst handles only what is left.

AI Agent — reads every document, confirms the data
Runs by itself on every individual's case in Data Confirming: {{ ai.model }} reads every document (the passport's two pages, the secondary ID and the proof of address) and fixed checks compare the read with the CRM. When all pass, a case the rules cleared is Verified with no analyst; a case with an alert left goes to Data Screening with the agent's watchlist finding (likely true match, possible match, or likely a different person), and an analyst clears the alert. When nothing else is wrong and no alert is left, it settles the record's own slips itself: a record that kept the father's name for the holder's, a married surname on the form, an English first name beside the naturalisation certificate (kept as the preferred name) or a PO Box for a home is corrected to what the documents agree on, then confirmed; a proof of address older than six months is asked for again (Resubmit POA), and the document linking two names or which home is the main one is asked for by RFI. Anything unreadable, expired, missing or different goes to an analyst with the reason. Companies, and cases a person already holds, are the analysts'.
